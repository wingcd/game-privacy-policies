## 二、游戏详细介绍（120-1500 字，约 560 字）

```text
《消个毛线》是一款轻松解压的毛线主题三消闯关游戏。

【核心玩法】
棋盘上铺满了五颜六色的毛线团和可爱小动物，点击即可将其收入下方的收纳格，集齐3个相同颜色的元素即可自动消除。收纳格数量有限，一旦被填满则挑战失败，因此每一步都要精打细算。关卡元素层层堆叠，优先消除表层、规划好收纳顺序是通关的关键。失败不用气馁，可消耗体力重新挑战，也可以通过复活继续当前进度。

【四大道具 助力通关】
移除：将收纳格中的毛线团移出，腾出宝贵空间；
交换：随机打乱棋盘上所有元素的位置，制造新的消除机会；
吸附：自动吸附一组可消除的同色元素，瞬间解围；
撤销：撤回上一步操作，随手补救失误。
卡关时观看激励视频即可获得道具，助你轻松过关。

【连胜挑战 奖励翻倍】
连续通关可积累连胜，连胜不仅能获得额外的收纳格子，还能提升金币加成倍率，最高可达5倍；一旦失败连胜中断，紧张与刺激并存。

【搜集羊毛 装修小屋】
通关搜集的羊毛可用于装修温馨小屋，解锁各式家具与装饰，打造属于你的治愈空间；还有丰富的形象与场景皮肤等你收集，让消除之旅焕然一新。

【丰富活动 惊喜不断】
七日签到、每日登录、每周挑战、存钱罐等福利活动轮番上阵；闯关榜、连胜榜、好友排行一较高下，还可以与好友分享装修成果。

海量精心设计的关卡与困难关卡持续更新，操作简单、上手即玩，适合在碎片时间放松心情。适度游戏益脑，沉迷游戏伤身，合理安排时间，享受健康生活。
```

## 四、英文版详细介绍（约 360 词）

```text
Yarn Match is a relaxing match-3 puzzle game with a cozy yarn theme.

[Core Gameplay]
The board is piled high with colorful yarn balls and adorable little animals. Tap one to send it into the slots below — collect three of the same color and they clear automatically. Slots are limited: fill every slot and the challenge fails, so every tap counts. Items stack up in layers, so clearing from the top and planning your picks is the key to winning. If you fail, spend one energy point to try again, or revive to pick up right where you left off.

[Four Boosters for Tough Levels]
Remove: move up to 3 yarn balls out of the slots and free up precious space;
Shuffle: randomly rearrange every animal on the board to open up new matches;
Clear: automatically clear one matching set and save the day;
Undo: take back your last move and fix any slip-up.
Stuck on a hard level? Watch a short video to earn a booster and sail through.

[Win Streaks, Bigger Rewards]
Clear levels back to back to build a Win Streak. Streaks earn you extra slots and raise your coin bonus — up to 5x. But one loss breaks the streak, so the tension never drops.

[Collect Yarn, Decorate Your Home]
The wool you collect from cleared levels can be spent on decorating a cozy little house — unlock furniture and decorations to create your own healing space. Plenty of character and scene skins are waiting to be collected to give your journey a fresh new look.

[Events Full of Surprises]
7-Day Sign-in, daily login rewards, weekly challenges, a piggy bank that fills up while you're away, and more. Climb the level, Win Streak, and friend leaderboards, and share your decorated home with friends.

With tons of carefully designed levels and hard modes added regularly, it's easy to pick up and perfect for unwinding in spare moments. Play in moderation and enjoy a healthy, balanced life.
```
